Privacy & Data Security

Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED // SEPTEMBER 1, 2026 • APPLICABLE PLATFORMS: META, YOUTUBE, TIKTOK

Our Plain-English Privacy Guarantee: We only use your social media permissions to upload your videos and reply to your fans. We never sell your data, we never see your personal passwords, and your content remains 100% yours.

01 // Who We Are & What This Policy Covers

SudoNath Flow and Nano Facts Automation ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by software engineer Nathaniel Faborada, provide automated social media cross-posting and community engagement tools. This policy explains what information we access when you connect your Facebook, YouTube, or TikTok accounts to our automation engine.

02 // What Information We Access (And Why)

We only request the bare minimum permissions needed to publish your content and keep your audience engaged:

  • Account Identifiers: We receive your public Channel ID, Page ID, or TikTok Open ID so our engine knows which account to publish your videos to.
  • Video Publishing Access: We use authorized API tokens to upload your video files and set titles, descriptions, and hashtags on your behalf.
  • Public Comment Notifications: We scan incoming comments on your published posts so our AI can generate helpful, friendly replies to your community.

03 // What We Never Do

To keep your accounts completely safe, we maintain strict security boundaries:

  • We never ask for or store your personal account passwords.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade your data or your fans' information to any third party.
  • We never publish unauthorized posts outside of your configured scheduling rules.

04 // How We Protect Your Tokens & Security

All connection keys and access tokens are encrypted and stored in secure cloud environments. They are accessed exclusively by automated background servers during scheduled posting windows and are never exposed to public browsers.

05 // Third-Party Platform Compliance

Our automation complies fully with official partner terms and developer requirements:

  • Meta Platform Terms and Community Guidelines
  • YouTube API Services Terms of Service & Google Privacy Policy
  • TikTok Developer Terms of Service and Content API Policies

06 // Questions & Support

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or need help with your connected accounts, you can reach out directly to lead engineer Nathaniel Faborada at: nathanielfaburada14@gmail.com or via Developer Portfolio.
